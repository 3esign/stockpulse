01How It's Played

Two ways to earn. Both pay straight into your wallet, on-chain, the moment they trigger — in real, tokenized shares, not SOL.

🏁 The launch race

The first buyers to claim a rank get an instant payout straight from the game pot — in the real stock token the pot holds. Rank #1 gets the biggest slice, rank #10 the smallest. It decays fast, and once the 10 ranks are gone, they're gone.

📈 The flip

Once the launch race is full, every trade — buy or sell — earns the trader a reward from the pot. Flip a trade (buy then sell, or the reverse) and the reward doubles. Trading against your own last move is the skill play.

02How It Actually Works

Everything below is independently verifiable on-chain — that's the entire point of building it this way.

What's actually new here

STOCK PULSE's reward pot holds and pays out a real, tokenized stock — a Token-2022 asset issued by a regulated third party (Backed Finance's xStocks line, e.g. Tesla as TSLAx), redeemable 1:1 against the real underlying share by the issuer. A rank-race at launch and an ongoing per-trade activity reward, both paid straight from a PDA-signed pot (Program-Derived Address — an address computed from the program's own ID, held by no one's private key), no admin keys, no manual claims.

The on-chain program

Built on Pinocchio, a minimal, no-heap Solana framework — real savings, since every byte of a deployed program is backed by a permanent, real rent deposit. It exposes three instructions:

  • init_launch — one-time setup: records the stock token's mint and the pot's reward parameters.
  • claim_rank — callable while ranks 1–10 are open; assigns the next rank and pays out immediately, straight from the pot, in the real stock token.
  • record_activity — callable on every trade once the race is full; credits activity and pays the flip-aware reward straight from the pot.

Every payout is a Token-2022 TransferChecked CPI signed by a program-owned PDA (Program-Derived Address — an address computed from the program's own ID, held by no one's private key). There is no admin instruction to drain the pot, pause payouts, or change the rules after deployment.

Honest disclosure: the stock token itself has real, disclosed centralization

The stock token the pot holds is not a permissionless native asset the way SOL is — it's issued and backed by a regulated third party, and that issuer keeps real, on-chain powers over it: a permanentDelegate that can move or burn any holder's tokens, and a pausableConfig that can freeze all transfers network-wide. Both are standard for a regulated, redeemable security token, not a bug in this program — but they're real, and worth knowing before you play. STOCK PULSE's own program has no such powers over the pot; this risk sits entirely with the token issuer, not with this game.

Permissionless by design

Any wallet can call claim_rank or record_activity directly — no allowlist, no backend approval, no API key. The program's on-chain bytecode is the only authority, and anyone can inspect it. Reward sizes are kept modest on purpose: a fully open, low-friction mechanism is easier to interact with at scale than a gated one, and that openness is an accepted economic tradeoff, not an oversight.
